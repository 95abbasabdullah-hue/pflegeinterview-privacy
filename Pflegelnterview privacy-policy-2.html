Privacy Policy for PflegeInterview

Last updated: 03 September 2026

PflegeInterview ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a mobile application designed to help users in Germany prepare for hospital and nursing ("Pflege") job interviews through practice questions, quizzes, and answers, available in German, Arabic, and English. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the PflegeInterview app available on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how the App is built and configured, we may collect the following:

  • Account information (if you create an account): name, email address, chosen language.
  • App usage data: quiz results, progress, scores, questions answered, session duration, and feature interactions.
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, unique device/app identifiers, language/locale settings, and crash/diagnostic logs.

Note: We do not collect any medical, health, or patient-related information. The App is an interview-preparation and quiz tool only; it does not provide medical advice and is not used to store or process any real patient or health data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's quiz and interview-practice features.
  • To save your progress and personalize content in your selected language (German, Arabic, or English).
  • To improve app performance, fix bugs, and understand feature usage through aggregated analytics.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you. Common examples include:

  • Google Play Services

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data. We recommend reviewing their policies for more detail.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the App (e.g. hosting, analytics), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data may be stored on servers located in Qatar / Middle East.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union / Germany, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide the App's features or as required by law. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

PflegeInterview is intended for individuals preparing for job interviews (typically adults) and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. International Users / Language Availability

The App is available in German, Arabic, and English. Regardless of the language you use, this Privacy Policy applies equally to all users of the App.
